GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Which links are supported?

Public TikTok video links that are directly accessible may work. Private, removed, restricted, or unavailable posts cannot be retrieved.

Do I need to sign in?

No ClipNest account is required. The service does not ask for your TikTok password or cookies.

Can I download any video?

Only download videos you created or have permission to save. You are responsible for respecting applicable laws and platform terms.

Why might a download fail?

Links can fail if a post is private, deleted, region-restricted, age-restricted, or no longer supported by the extraction engine.
